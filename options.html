Hemline

Everything stays in your browser. Your resume and the job description are sent only to the AI provider whose key you add below.

  1. Add an AI key
  2. Import your resume
  3. Fill in your profile

1. AI keys

Keys are tried top to bottom; if one fails or runs out of quota, Hemline moves to the next. Claude needs a paid Anthropic API key (a Claude.ai subscription does not work here). Gemini, Groq and Cerebras have free tiers.

2. Your resume

Paste your resume as plain text and Hemline converts it once into its LaTeX template. Already have a LaTeX resume in Jake's template? Paste or upload it directly.

Project pool and skill pool (optional, improves tailoring)

Project pool: extra \resumeProjectHeading blocks. Hemline picks the 3 that best fit each job.

Skill pool: every skill you would be willing to list, comma-separated. Hemline pulls the ones each job asks for.

3. Profile

Used for your resume header, cover letters, and ATS auto-fill. Blank fields are never auto-filled.
