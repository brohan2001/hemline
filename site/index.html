JSeek Enhancer

A browser extension that tailors your LaTeX resume and cover letter to any job posting in about 30 seconds, using your own Claude key or a free Gemini, Groq, or Cerebras key.

  • Adds Resume, Cover Letter, Message, and Email buttons to LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, and more.
  • Picks the skills and projects that match each job, keeps the resume to one page, and reports an ATS keyword-match score.
  • Compiles LaTeX to PDF inside your browser. No server, no account.
  • Fills application forms from your profile and remembers where you have applied.
  • Includes a live feed of new US tech postings from 2,400+ company job boards.
